## 1. The market has already moved

AI is rapidly becoming a mainstream way people research, compare, learn and make decisions.

Buyers are increasingly asking AI about products, suppliers, technologies, expertise and solutions before they ever reach a company's website or speak to its sales team.

The question is no longer whether AI search will matter. The question is what AI is already saying about your business, and whether you are part of the answer.

Recent market evidence shows the speed of this change. In Switzerland, dedicated AI platforms are already being used for search more than YouTube or social media. For complex B2B organisations, this represents a fundamental change in how buyers discover and evaluate businesses.

## 2. The B2B dilemma

Complex B2B organisations possess exactly the knowledge AI needs: technical documentation, product information, research, processes, presentations, training materials and specialist expertise.

But much of it remains trapped inside complex documents, systems and people's heads, or communicated in formats that were never designed for an AI-first world.

This creates both a visibility problem and a missing-content problem.

Existing knowledge needs to become easier for AI to discover, understand and trust. At the same time, important products, processes and areas of expertise may simply not have enough useful digital communication around them for AI or buyers to draw on.

Your buyers can now ask AI questions about your market, your products and your competitors without ever asking you. If AI does not have sufficient credible knowledge about your organisation, someone else's expertise can become the answer.

Binumi solves both sides of this problem: improving the AI visibility of existing knowledge and creating the missing communications that should exist.

Crucially, the video we create is not simply designed for human engagement or produced more cost-effectively. Binumi creates authoritative video knowledge assets specifically for an AI-first discovery environment, combining the communication itself with carefully curated supporting knowledge so that the subject, expertise and evidence within it can be more effectively discovered and understood by AI.

Because Binumi can do this at enterprise scale and at a radically different cost base from traditional production, organisations can systematically fill knowledge gaps rather than relying on occasional high-cost video projects.

## 3. The opportunity for complex industries

The explosion of generic, synthetic and mass-produced AI content creates an unusual opportunity for organisations operating in technical, regulated and high-trust markets.

Their advantage is not the ability to create more content. Their advantage is that they possess knowledge that is difficult to manufacture: genuine expertise, technical evidence, experienced people, specialist processes, customer knowledge and first-party insight.

In the AI era, knowledge becomes a competitive advantage when machines can discover, understand and trust it.

This gives complex B2B organisations the opportunity to turn the depth and specificity of their knowledge into an advantage over generic AI content.

## 4. Video as an AI visibility engine

Video is no longer only a communication asset. Properly created and structured, it can become a powerful AI knowledge and discovery asset.

This is particularly important for complex B2B organisations. Their products are technical. Their processes are complicated. Their documentation is dense. Much of their expertise sits with specialists. Video can make this knowledge understandable without stripping away its depth.

Binumi creates video with this new discovery environment in mind. The video communicates clearly to people, while the knowledge surrounding it is deliberately prepared so that AI can more effectively understand the subjects, entities, expertise and evidence it contains.

These knowledge assets can then work across the organisation's wider digital presence and appropriate external environments, increasing the number of credible surfaces through which that expertise can be discovered and verified.

The result is a dual-purpose asset: video that engages and converts people while strengthening the knowledge available to AI.

Independent research supports the direction of travel. YouTube is already a meaningful AI citation surface, and research on cited videos shows that structured chapters can create multiple extractable citation points in Google's AI surfaces. More broadly, academic GEO research has demonstrated that optimisation techniques can materially improve visibility in generative-engine responses. These findings support the opportunity, but do not justify promising guaranteed citations or rankings.

## 5. The Binumi proposition

Binumi helps complex organisations understand and improve how AI sees their business.

We begin with the Binumi AI Visibility Assessment, providing a credible view of how effectively a page's digital knowledge can be understood, trusted and surfaced by AI.

But the assessment is only the instrument. The value is what we do next.

Binumi identifies what AI can see, where important knowledge is weak or missing, and where video can materially strengthen the organisation's digital presence.

We improve what exists. We create what is missing. And we turn complex products, documents, expertise and processes into AI-ready video knowledge assets at enterprise scale.

Binumi's combination of intelligent optimisation and purpose-built video is designed to drive greater AI visibility, credibility, engagement and conversion.

## 7. The commercial conversation and ICP

This is a C-suite and senior leadership conversation, not primarily a conversation for marketers, creators or content producers.

Our primary ICP includes leaders responsible for revenue, growth, customer engagement, operations, workforce capability, knowledge and enterprise performance. Depending on the organisation, this can include CEOs, CROs, COOs, CFOs, People Directors, business-unit leaders and Operating Partners.

The conversation therefore does not begin with content production, SEO or even GEO. It begins with business exposure.

> "Your customers are already asking AI about your market, your products and your competitors. Do you know what it is telling them?"

Then: "Does AI have access to enough credible knowledge about your organisation to give the right answer?"

And finally: "Where that knowledge is missing, how quickly and cost-effectively can your organisation create it?"

This is where Binumi becomes the solution, not simply the diagnostic.

The AI Visibility Assessment provides the evidence. The meeting provides the diagnosis. Binumi provides the solution.

Expose the risk → Measure the position → Diagnose the opportunity → Improve what exists → Create what is missing → Amplify through AI-ready video → Measure again

## 8. Disclosure discipline

Public positioning explains the problem, the urgency, the opportunity and why Binumi is qualified to solve it. It does not explain the mechanism.

We can publicly state that Binumi creates video designed to strengthen AI visibility as well as human engagement, and that our approach combines intelligent optimisation, structured knowledge and appropriate distribution.

We should not publicly disclose the specific metadata methodology, knowledge structures, publishing architecture, distribution locations, verification strategy or processes used to achieve those outcomes.

The grader exposes the problem. The score earns the conversation. The meeting reveals the diagnosis. The proposal reveals the solution.

If someone could reconstruct our solution from our public explanation, we have said too much.

## 9. The language we own

Our external language should remain commercial rather than technical.

AI visibility. Buyer discovery. Expertise. Trust. Authority. Video. Knowledge. Engagement. Conversion. Competitive advantage.

GEO describes the category, but it should not become the proposition.

Our customer does not fundamentally need GEO. They need their business, products and expertise to be understood, trusted and recommended when their buyers ask AI.

## 10. The core positioning

Your buyers are asking AI what to buy, who to trust and where to learn, and right now AI is answering for you.

Binumi helps complex B2B organisations understand how effectively they are being represented, identify the knowledge AI is missing, and strengthen their AI presence through intelligent optimisation and purpose-built video.

If AI can't understand your expertise, it can't recommend your business.

## 11. The urgency

This is not another digital marketing trend. It is a fundamental change in how buyers discover, research and evaluate organisations.

Complex B2B organisations cannot assume that traditional search authority, existing websites or occasional content production will automatically translate into AI visibility.

Their buyers are already changing behaviour. Their competitors are becoming part of AI-generated answers. And many organisations have little visibility into what AI understands about them or where important knowledge is absent.

Yet they already possess much of what they need to compete: expertise, evidence, people, documents, products, processes and existing video.

Binumi can make that existing knowledge more valuable to AI and create the missing video knowledge assets required to fill the gaps, continuously, compliantly and at enterprise scale.

The companies that act now have an opportunity to establish authority while this discovery landscape is still forming. Those that wait risk allowing competitors to define the answers for them.

Your buyers are already asking AI.

Binumi shows you what AI sees and what's missing.

Then we improve what exists and create the AI-ready video knowledge that helps your business become part of the answer.
